
# Kinetix AI - Hackathon Demo Video Script (Under 3 Minutes)

This script is a blueprint for a winning video. It tells a clear story, showcases the app's most impressive features, and directly addresses the hackathon's judging criteria by highlighting the deep integration with the Gemini 3 API.

**Target Length:** 2:45 - 3:00 minutes
**Music:** Start with a slightly tense, focused track. Transition to an inspiring, modern, and energetic beat when the solution is revealed.

---

### **Part 1: The Problem & The Hook (0:00 - 0:20)**

**Goal:** Grab the judges' attention with a relatable problem.

*   **(0:00 - 0:10) Visual:**
    *   Start with a black screen. A single line of text fades in: *"Every year, millions are injured exercising."*
    *   Quick, sharp cuts of stock footage (or self-recorded clips) showing common, subtle exercise mistakes:
        *   A person's back rounding slightly on a deadlift.
        *   A person's knees caving inward on a squat.
        *   A person shrugging their shoulders during a push-up.
    *   **Style:** Use a slightly desaturated or dark color grade. Add a subtle, tense sound effect with each cut.

*   **(0:10 - 0:20) Visual:**
    *   The shot transitions to you, looking at your laptop with a slightly confused or uncertain expression.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "Good form is everything. But how do you know if you're doing it right?"
    *   **Voiceover (Your own voice, clear and authentic):** "I love fitness, but I've seen too many people, including myself, get hurt from simple mistakes. A personal trainer is the answer, but they're expensive. I knew there had to be a better way."

---

### **Part 2: The Solution - Introducing Kinetix AI (0:20 - 0:45)**

**Goal:** Introduce your app as the hero.

*   **(0:20 - 0:35) Visual:**
    *   The music shifts to become more uplifting and hopeful.
    *   The screen shows the beautiful Kinetix AI **Idle Screen**. Your hand enters the frame and confidently clicks "START SESSION".
    *   The active session screen fades in, showing your webcam feed.
    *   **Voiceover:** "So I built Kinetix AI. It's a personal trainer powered by Google's Gemini 3 API that runs right in your browser. It uses your webcam to analyze your movement and give you real-time, expert feedback."

*   **(0:35 - 0:45) Visual:**
    *   Show a clean, animated diagram illustrating the core concept.
    *   **Left Side:** Icon of a webcam sending video frames.
    *   **Middle:** The Gemini 3 Logo with the text "Multimodal Analysis".
    *   **Right Side:** Icons for "Visual Feedback," "Audio Cues," and "Written Reports."
    *   **Voiceover:** "It sees what you're doing, understands the biomechanics, and coaches you to perfection."

---

### **Part 3: The Magic - Real-Time Coaching Demo (0:45 - 1:45)**

**Goal:** This is the "wow" moment. Show the core feedback loop in action.

*   **(0:45 - 1:00) - Good Form (`GREEN`)**
    *   **Visual:** You are performing squats with perfect form. The screen border has a **soft green glow**. The holographic skeleton is thin and green. The **Status Badge** reads "GOOD FORM".
    *   The **Speech Text Overlay** animates in: `"Perfect. Make the next one look exactly like that."`
    *   **Sound:** Play the clear, generated audio of the coaching cue.
    *   **Voiceover:** "When your form is good, Kinetix encourages you, reinforcing that perfect rep."

*   **(1:00 - 1:15) - Minor Correction (`YELLOW`)**
    *   **Visual:** On the next squat, your knees cave in slightly. The screen's glow instantly shifts to **yellow**. The skeleton becomes thicker. The **Status Badge** reads "ALMOST THERE".
    *   The **Speech Text Overlay** appears: `"Push your knees out sideways!"`
    *   **Sound:** Play the corresponding audio cue.
    *   **Voiceover:** "If you make a small mistake, the AI coach gives you a simple, actionable cue to get you back on track."

*   **(1:15 - 1:30) - Dangerous Form (`RED`) & The "Brain"**
    *   **Visual:** Your back starts to round. The screen pulses with an urgent **red glow**. The skeleton is thick and red. The **Status Badge** pulses "CORRECTION NEEDED".
    *   The **Speech Text Overlay** appears: `"Careful! Keep your chest up."`
    *   **HERE'S THE KEY PART:** As this happens, quickly animate the **"Brain" Sidebar** open to the **"Pro Log"**. Highlight a line of the technical `thought_signature` like: `> Thoracic kyphosis detected.`
    *   **Voiceover:** "And if your form becomes dangerous, it intervenes immediately. But this isn't just a simple detector. Kinetix has two minds: an internal Analyst that thinks in precise, technical terms..."

*   **(1:30 - 1:45) - The "Coach"**
    *   **Visual:** Collapse the Pro Log and switch the sidebar to the **"Coach Log"**. Show the simple cues scrolling by.
    *   **Voiceover:** "...and an external Coach that translates that analysis into simple language anyone can understand. This dual-persona is only possible because of Gemini's advanced reasoning."

---

### **Part 4: Closing the Loop - The Intelligent Summary (1:45 - 2:30)**

**Goal:** Show how Kinetix provides value even after the workout is over.

*   **(1:45 - 2:00) Visual:**
    *   You finish your set, looking tired but accomplished. You click "END SESSION".
    *   The "Generating Summary..." screen appears.
    *   **Voiceover:** "But the coaching doesn't stop when your workout does."

*   **(2:00 - 2:30) Visual:**
    *   The **Session Summary** screen animates in.
    *   Use your mouse cursor to highlight the different sections as you talk about them.
    *   First, highlight the stats (duration, form score).
    *   Then, move to the AI's qualitative `overall_assessment`.
    *   Finally, and most importantly, **zoom in on the "Recommended Exercises" section**.
    *   **Voiceover:** "Kinetix uses Gemini to generate a full summary of your performance, highlighting your strengths and pinpointing areas for improvement. Then, it goes one step further. Using a chained prompt, it takes those areas for improvement and generates a personalized list of corrective exercises just for you, closing the feedback loop and giving you a clear path to get better."

---

### **Part 5: Conclusion & Vision (2:30 - 2:55)**

**Goal:** Summarize the project and leave a lasting impression.

*   **(2:30 - 2:45) Visual:**
    *   Show a final, polished shot of the Kinetix UI.
    *   On top of it, overlay the key Gemini features you used:
        *   **Text Overlay:** `Built with Gemini 3`
        *   **Bullet Points:** `Multimodal Vision Input`, `Advanced Reasoning (Dual-Persona)`, `Chained Prompts`, `Reliable JSON Output`, `Text-to-Speech`
    *   **Voiceover:** "I built Kinetix to make expert coaching accessible to everyone. By leveraging the full power of the Gemini 3 API—from its multimodal vision to its sophisticated reasoning and chained-prompt capabilities—Kinetix isn't just an app; it's a true AI partner in your fitness journey."

*   **(2:45 - 2:55) Visual:**
    *   Fade to a black screen with the Kinetix logo.
    *   **Text on screen:**
        *   Kinetix AI
        *   Prevent Injury. Perfect Your Technique. Unlock Your Potential.
        *   [Link to your project]
    *   **Voiceover (passionate):** "This is the future of personal training. Thank you."
